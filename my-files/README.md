# my-files — your private space

This folder holds the estimates you paste and the reports MechanicLens returns. It is git-ignored in full. A fresh clone protects your files the moment you drop them in.

What lands here:

- Your redacted estimate text.
- A saved report, if you want one.
- Notes on what the shop said.

Rules:

- Never commit anything from this folder.
- Redact your name, address, phone, VIN, plate, and account digits before saving.
- The kit does not need your personal data to decode the estimate.
