# What MechanicLens is

## The artifact

A Markdown-only kit. Paste the spine into any chat, then paste the estimate and the symptom story. The kit returns a reading, labels, questions, and a close (documentation habit, no verdict). No install, no account, no network.

## The seven passes

1. **Anatomy** — map parts, labor, fees, taxes, total.
2. **Symptom check** — does the quoted work match the complaint?
3. **Label pass** — Standard / Check / Question this, per line, with the reason quoted.
4. **Script pass** — 3–6 counter questions, each with a "good answer" guide.
5. **Decision pass** — approve / decline / second opinion, plus the documentation habit.
6. **After pass** — invoice versus estimate, when both exist.
7. **Output** — the reading, the table, the questions, the checklist.

## Labels

- **Standard** — normal and clear.
- **Check** — normal but something is unstated.
- **Question this** — unclear or unusual enough to ask about.

No scores. No percentages. No probability. No verdict.

## Document types

- **Repair estimate** — primary, v0.1.
- **Final invoice** — after-repair branch, v0.2.
- **Body-shop estimate** — candidate, not scoped.

## Refusals

- No prices, price ranges, or "what this should cost."
- No diagnosis, repair advice, or safety assessment.
- No accusation. "Question this" is the ceiling.
- No negotiation script. The questions are for comprehension.
- No fetching, clicking, or transmitting.
- No verdict. The user decides.

## Non-goals

A price database, a shop-review site, an OBD tool, a repair manual, or a legal-advice service. Those are different products with different failure modes.
