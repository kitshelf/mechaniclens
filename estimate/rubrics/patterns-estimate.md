# Estimate pattern library

**Status: draft.** The patterns below are seeded from the design brief. Each needs a source and a dated "Verified as of" line before the kit ships. Until then, treat this as a working draft, not a calibrated library.

The rule for every pattern: it fires only when the document's own wording supports it. If the wording is ambiguous, label the line **Check** and ask the question; do not guess.

## Fees

| ID | Pattern | What to look for | Question to ask | Not a pattern when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F-1 | Diagnostic fee | A separate charge for diagnosis; the estimate does not say whether it is credited if the repair is approved. | "Is the diagnostic fee credited toward the repair if I approve it?" | The estimate states the fee is credited, or the diagnosis is included in the repair price. |
| F-2 | Shop supplies | A percentage or flat fee for supplies; the estimate does not say what it covers or whether it is capped. | "What does the shop-supplies charge cover, and is it a fixed percentage of labor?" | The fee is itemized and capped, or the estimate explains exactly what it buys. |
| F-3 | Environmental or hazmat fee | A charge for hazardous-waste handling; the estimate does not say which materials or why. | "Which materials does the environmental fee cover for this job?" | The fee is tied to a specific material or disposal item on the estimate. |
| F-4 | Disposal fee | A charge to dispose of old parts or fluids; the estimate does not say what is being disposed of. | "What is being disposed of, and is the disposal fee already included in the parts or labor line?" | The fee is specific and not duplicated elsewhere. |
| F-5 | Administrative or document fee | A fee for paperwork, filing, or "shop administration" with no stated basis. | "What does the administrative fee cover?" | The fee is a documented regulatory or registration charge. |
| F-6 | Storage fee | A daily or flat storage charge; the estimate does not say when it starts or why the vehicle is stored. | "When does storage start, and what is the daily rate?" | The vehicle is stored at the customer's request or the fee is waived. |
| F-7 | "Misc" or "other" | A catch-all line with no description. | "What specific work or part does the 'misc' line cover?" | The line has a description elsewhere on the estimate. |

## Labor

| ID | Pattern | What to look for | Question to ask | Not a pattern when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-1 | Book time versus actual time | Labor is billed in hours; the estimate does not say whether the hours are book time or actual time. | "Are these hours book time or actual time?" | The estimate states the basis, or the job is a fixed-price operation. |
| L-2 | Labor rate not shown | The labor total appears without an hourly rate. | "What is the labor rate, and how many hours are on this line?" | The estimate shows the rate and hours. |
| L-3 | Overlapping labor | Two labor lines appear to cover the same access or removal. | "Do these two labor lines overlap, or is each a separate operation?" | The lines are clearly separate operations on different systems. |
| L-4 | Diagnosis labor | A labor charge for diagnosis separate from the repair labor. | "Is the diagnosis labor separate from the repair labor, or is it included?" | The estimate states diagnosis is included. |
| L-5 | Add-on labor | Labor for work beyond the symptom the customer described. | "Was this work authorized, and what symptom led to it?" | The add-on is safety-critical and the estimate explains why. |
| L-6 | Remove-and-reinstall charge | A labor line for removing and reinstalling a part that the same job already requires. | "Is the remove-and-reinstall charge already included in the main labor line?" | The estimate shows it as a separate, necessary operation. |

## Parts

| ID | Pattern | What to look for | Question to ask | Not a pattern when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-1 | Markup tier not shown | A part price with no indication of OEM, aftermarket, remanufactured, or salvage. | "Is this part OEM, aftermarket, remanufactured, or salvage?" | The estimate names the part type. |
| P-2 | Duplicate parts line | The same part appears twice, or two lines describe the same part. | "Are these two lines the same part, or two different parts?" | The lines are clearly different part numbers or quantities. |
| P-3 | Quantity mismatch | The quantity on the line does not match the job description. | "How many of this part does the job require?" | The quantity is consistent with the described work. |
| P-4 | Kit price without breakdown | A kit or assembly is priced as one line with no individual parts. | "What is included in the kit, and can you show the individual parts?" | The kit is a sealed assembly that cannot be broken down. |
| P-5 | Part number missing | A part line has no part number. | "What is the part number for this line?" | The estimate includes the part number or a clear description. |
| P-6 | OEM/aftermarket ambiguity | The estimate uses a brand name without saying whether the part is OEM or aftermarket. | "Is this the OEM part or an aftermarket equivalent?" | The estimate states the part type. |

## Scope

| ID | Pattern | What to look for | Question to ask | Not a pattern when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S-1 | Symptom mismatch | The quoted work does not match the symptom the customer described. | "The car is doing [symptom]; does this work address that?" | The estimate explains the link between the symptom and the work. |
| S-2 | Recommended versus required | The estimate presents maintenance or preventive work as required. | "Is this work required to fix the problem, or is it recommended maintenance?" | The estimate states the work is required for safety or the repair. |
| S-3 | Deferred maintenance bundled | Several maintenance items are bundled into one job without separate approval. | "Can we separate the maintenance items from the repair?" | The items are part of a single repair procedure. |
| S-4 | Safety inspection add-on | A safety inspection or check is added to the repair without a stated finding. | "What did the inspection find, and is this work required because of it?" | The inspection found a specific safety issue named on the estimate. |
| S-5 | "While we're in there" | Additional work is proposed because the vehicle is already apart. | "Is this work necessary now, or can it wait?" | The additional work is required to complete the repair correctly. |

## Documentation

| ID | Pattern | What to look for | Question to ask | Not a pattern when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-1 | No validity date | The estimate has no "valid until" or "good through" date. | "How long is this estimate valid?" | The estimate states a validity period. |
| D-2 | No authorization line | The estimate has no place for the customer to approve or decline. | "Where do I authorize the work, and what am I approving?" | The estimate includes an authorization section. |
| D-3 | Total does not add up | The line totals do not sum to the stated total. | "Can you show how the total was calculated?" | The total matches the lines, including tax and fees. |
| D-4 | Tax or fee base unclear | Tax or a fee is applied without saying what it applies to. | "What is the tax or fee applied to?" | The estimate states the base. |
| D-5 | Verbal authorization noted | The estimate says work was authorized verbally, with no written approval. | "Can you send the written authorization for the added work?" | The estimate includes a written approval. |

## Using the library

1. Read the document's anatomy first. Do not label before you have the lines.
2. Apply only the patterns the wording supports. A pattern that almost fits is a **Check**, not a **Question this**.
3. Quote the line with every label.
4. Check the *not-a-pattern-when* column before firing. A deadline, a fee, or a markup is not a marker by itself; the question is whether the document explains it.
5. A clean estimate should produce mostly **Standard** labels. If every line is flagged, the kit is not calibrated.
