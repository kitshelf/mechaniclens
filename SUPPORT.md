# Support

MechanicLens is a Markdown kit. There is no install, no account, and no backend.

## Before you ask

- Read the [user guide](USER-GUIDE.md) for the ninety-second walkthrough.
- Read the [charter](WHAT-THIS-IS.md) for what the kit refuses to do. Price questions and diagnosis requests are out of scope by design.

## Where to ask

- **A bug or a confusing report** — open an issue on the repository with the estimate text redacted. Do not paste your name, address, phone, VIN, plate, or policy numbers.
- **A pattern the kit misses** — use the [pattern submission template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/pattern-submission.md). Include the exact wording, a *not-a-pattern-when* line, and a source.
- **A security problem** — follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.

## What support cannot do

The kit cannot tell you what a repair should cost, whether a shop is honest, whether a repair is safe, or what to do about a dispute. It decodes a document. For a dispute, contact your state's consumer-protection office or a local consumer-aid organization; the kit names channels but does not advise or file for you.
