# The after-repair branch

Run this branch only when the work is done and the user has both the approved estimate and the final invoice. It is a comparison, not a new verdict.

## What to compare

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Authorized work | Does each invoice line appear on the estimate you approved? |
| Added work | Which invoice lines were not on the estimate? Was the addition authorized in writing, by phone, or not at all? |
| Parts | Do the part numbers, quantities, and descriptions match? |
| Labor | Do the labor hours and rate match the estimate? |
| Fees | Did supplies, environmental, hazmat, disposal, or shop fees change? |
| Total | Does the invoice total equal the estimate total plus any authorized additions? |

## What to do with a difference

1. Ask the shop, in writing, to explain each line that differs. Quote the line and the estimate it came from.
2. Keep the approved estimate, the final invoice, and any messages about added work.
3. If the difference is not resolved, contact your state or local consumer-protection office and ask what process applies. The kit names the channel; it does not file anything for you.
4. If the work was financed or paid by card, ask the lender or card issuer what dispute process applies. The kit does not give legal or financial advice.

## What the kit will not do

- It will not say the shop cheated.
- It will not calculate what the invoice "should" be.
- It will not draft a demand letter or a legal notice.
- It will not file a complaint, a chargeback, or a claim.

The kit reads the documents and hands over the comparison. The user decides what to do next.
